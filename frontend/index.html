6 GB VRAM + 32 GB System RAM Local Setup Guide & ComfyUI Workflow

Download ComfyUI 6GB Workflow (.JSON)

Your 32 GB System RAM is a superpower on a 6 GB GPU: Pixal3D Multi-View caches ~12 GiB of multi-view DINOv3 NAF features during conditioning. Passing --lowvram --disable-pinned-memory --reserve-vram 1.0 --cache-none keeps active GPU memory at ~5.5–5.9 GB VRAM while streaming the 12 GiB cache cleanly from your 32 GB RAM without triggering CUDA pinned-memory crashes! 

# Step 1: Download MIT-licensed Comfy-Org/Pixal3D INT8 + VAEs + BiRefNet (plus optional Q4_K_M GGUF)
python scripts/download_models_6gb.py --profile pixal3d --comfy-dir "C:/ComfyUI"

# Step 2: Launch ComfyUI (>= 0.33.0) with our 6 GB VRAM + 32 GB RAM Offload Launcher
# Windows:
scripts\start_comfyui_6gb.bat
# Linux:
./scripts/start_comfyui_6gb.sh

# Step 3: Launch RoForge 3D Web App (Auto-connects to ComfyUI on port 8188!)
scripts\start_creator.bat
